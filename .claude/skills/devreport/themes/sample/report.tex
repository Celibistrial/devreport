\bigskip

tidepool runs background jobs for our Node services out of one Postgres table. We built it to replace a Redis queue that lost jobs on every failover, and to keep the job history in the same database as the rest of our data.

\section{How it works}

Each job is a row. Workers claim rows with \texttt{SELECT \dots{} FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED}, so two workers never take the same job and no lock server is needed.

\begin{itemize}
  \item Failed jobs back off exponentially and retry up to five times.
  \item A small Go sidecar moves jobs past the retry cap into a review queue.
  \item The dashboard reads the same table, so there is no second store to keep in sync.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Latency}

We tuned the claim query and the index on \texttt{run\_at} over eight weeks. The p95 time from enqueue to start fell from 212 ms to 104 ms.

\section{Failures}

Most failed jobs were timeouts in calls to a slow payment API. Raising its timeout and moving it to its own worker pool removed about half of them.
